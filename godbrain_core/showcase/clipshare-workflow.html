03 / SHARE

The selected file. Not the library.

Copy its exact ready link.
Check live expiry and revocation.
Serve read-only media or a minimal player.

Hash-bound receipt / live share state / optional restricted HTTPS
A receipt proves preparation. It does not grant perpetual access.

Owner administration stays local. Revocation stops future access, not copies already downloaded. Public reachability needs its own external check.
